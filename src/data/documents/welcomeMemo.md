**To:** Incoming Pool Management Team
**From:** Board of Governors
**Re:** Welcome to {{poolName}}

---

On behalf of the Board of Governors, welcome to {{poolName}}.

You are assuming responsibility for the Pool as it enters the {{startingYear}} policy year. During the
years ahead, you will oversee the Pool's financial and operating strategy and make decisions that will
affect its members, its financial position, and its ability to meet its obligations over the long term.

{{poolName}} currently serves {{enrolledMemberCount}} of {{totalMemberCount}} eligible public entities,
representing approximately {{enrolledPayroll}} in payroll and {{enrolledTIV}} in total insured value. The
Pool closed the most recent year with approximately {{latestEndingSurplus}} in surplus and a
premium-to-surplus ratio of {{premiumToSurplusRatio}}. Additional information regarding the Pool's
membership, recent financial results, claims experience, and coverage lines is included in the reports
provided to you.

## Coverage Provided by the Pool

{{poolName}} currently provides {{selectedCoverageLines}} coverage. Each active coverage line has its own
exposures, claim patterns, loss development characteristics, and financial considerations. Management
should evaluate the performance of each line individually while also considering its effect on the overall
financial condition of the Pool.

The Pool exists to provide its members with dependable coverage at a reasonable and stable cost.
Accomplishing that objective requires more than simply producing a favorable financial result in any one
year. Rates must remain adequate, risks must be selected responsibly, claims must be funded, sufficient
liquidity must be maintained, and surplus must remain strong enough to protect the organization when
experience is worse than expected.

## The Management Cycle

As the management team, you will establish the Pool's strategy one year at a time. At the beginning of
each year, you will review the Pool's financial results, claims activity, reserve development, membership,
investment performance, and other significant developments. You will also receive reports and
recommendations from the Pool's professional staff.

Based on that information, you will make the management decisions that will guide the Pool through the
coming year. These decisions may include changes to member funding rates, underwriting and enrollment
standards, investment allocation, and other areas of financial and risk management.

Once those decisions have been made, another year of operations will unfold. Members may join or leave the
Pool. New claims will occur, and older claims may develop differently than originally expected.
Investments may perform better or worse than anticipated. Economic conditions, legal developments,
catastrophes, or other unexpected events may also affect the Pool.

The results of each year become the starting point for the next.

This is important because many of the decisions you make will not reveal their full consequences
immediately. An inadequate funding rate may appear manageable before adverse loss development emerges.
Rapid growth may strengthen contribution volume while also increasing the amount of risk supported by the
Pool's surplus. A more aggressive investment allocation may improve returns in favorable markets while
creating additional financial pressure during an unfavorable year.

## Evaluating Multiple Lines

If your Pool provides more than one coverage line, each line should be evaluated on its own merits. Your
Pool currently provides {{selectedCoverageLines}}. These lines may respond differently to changes in loss
experience, economic conditions, member growth, and other events. Strong performance in one line can
offset deterioration in another when viewing only the Pool-wide results, so management should review both
the individual coverage-line results and the Pool as a whole.

The Pool's reports are intended to help you identify these differences. Pool-wide financial statements
provide an overall view of the organization, while coverage-line reports allow you to evaluate the
underlying performance of each active line. Claims and reserve information provide additional insight into
whether past expectations are continuing to hold.

## Your Departments

You will be supported by the Pool's Actuarial, Claims, Underwriting, and Finance departments. Each will
provide its best assessment based on the information available. Their recommendations should be considered
carefully, but none should be viewed as certain. Loss estimates change, assumptions are revised, financial
markets move, and unexpected events occur.

The **Actuarial Department** will assist in evaluating loss trends, funding needs, and the adequacy of
current contribution levels.

The **Claims Department** will provide information regarding recent losses, significant claims, and the
development of outstanding claim liabilities.

The **Underwriting Department** will provide insight into the Pool's membership, new applicants, renewals,
and the changing risk profile of the book.

The **Finance Department** will provide information regarding the Pool's financial position, liquidity,
investments, and other matters affecting its assets and surplus.

Each department views the Pool from a different perspective. It is the responsibility of the management
team to consider these perspectives together when establishing the Pool's overall strategy.

## The Board's Expectations

The Board of Governors does not expect management to eliminate risk. Risk is inherent in operating a public
entity risk pool. We do expect you to understand the risks being taken, consider both the immediate and
long-term effects of your decisions, and maintain the financial strength necessary to withstand difficult
years.

Success will not be determined by a single year's surplus or by maximizing any one financial measure. The
Board will look to your ability to balance member affordability, adequate funding, responsible growth,
financial stability, and the Pool's long-term ability to serve its members.

We wish you success as you assume responsibility for {{poolName}}.

**Board of Governors**
