Pricing & growth
Rate changelive revenue vs. retention

Multiplies the charged premium rate. Does not change expected losses — pure premium moves independently. A rate increase directly reduces satisfaction and retention; a decrease attracts new members.

Underwriting strictnesslive growth speed vs. book quality

Filters the new-member candidate pool toward higher risk quality above a threshold. Improves average risk quality (and therefore loss predictability) over time, at the cost of slower growth.

Funding confidence level (CLF)live price competitiveness vs. pricing safety margin

Loads an actuarial safety margin into the charged rate. Higher CLF means pricing is built around a more conservative loss estimate — costs more, but only affects the rate, not booked reserves.

Risk management
Risk control %live cash now vs. lower losses later

A direct expense today that slowly builds effectiveness over a multi-year lag, then reduces future expected losses. Effectiveness decays fast — especially if spending drops near zero — making this a real multi-year commitment, not a one-off toggle.

Reinsurance level & basislive basis: designed cost vs. protection from tail losses

Level sets retention/attachment, cost, and recovery. Basis (aggregate / occurrence / combined) determines whether protection responds to the year's total losses, a single large claim, or both — occurrence coverage is what catches one catastrophic claim that an aggregate-only program might let through untouched.

Reserve strategydesigned reported surplus now vs. future adverse-development risk

Preset (aggressive / moderate / conservative / very conservative) sets carried reserve as a multiplier on the indicated reserve. Thinner reserves boost this year's surplus but leave less room to absorb adverse development or a legislative shock later — and drive IRIS ratios 11/12/13 directly.

Direct expense that returns cash to members. Strongly boosts satisfaction and retention, but reduces this year's surplus and future investable assets. A line that just took an adverse development hit probably shouldn't be paying a big one.

Event-triggered decisions — only appear when a condition is met
Authorize inter-line loandesigned debt + interest vs. a visibly deficient line

Appears only when a line's ending surplus is negative.

Authorizing borrows from the shared pool at that year's realized investment return, fixed at origination, bringing the line back to zero. Declining leaves the line's surplus negative — likely blocking its dividend next year and flagging on capital adequacy / IRIS views.

Line surplusCapital adequacy statusDividend eligibility
Loan repayment aggressivenessdesigned faster debt payoff vs. faster surplus rebuild

Appears only while that line has an outstanding inter-line loan balance.

Sets what share of that line's positive net income repays the loan before flowing to its own surplus. High aggressiveness clears debt fast but freezes that line's own growth (and likely its dividend) longer than a gentler repayment pace would.
